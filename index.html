FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to buy stock upfront?

No. There is no minimum order quantity. Products are made when your customer orders, so you pay only when you sell.

What can I customise?

Oversized t-shirts (14 colours), sweatshirts (4), hoodies (7), tank tops (7) and acid-wash baby tops (3), plus full cut & sew. You choose the colour, print or embroidery, neck labels, tags and packaging.

What fabrics and sizes do you offer?

Oversized tees are 240 GSM polycotton terry in XS–XL. Sweatshirts and hoodies are 330 GSM polycotton fleece in S–XXL. Tank tops and baby tops are 240 GSM cotton terry. All fits are unisex.

Which printing methods do you use?

Screen printing, puff printing, DTF, DTG, sublimation and embroidery, all done in-house so quality stays consistent.

Where are the products made?

Fabric, cutting, stitching, printing, embroidery and packing all happen under one roof at our unit in Nelamangala, Bengaluru. We produce up to 3,000 pieces a day and ship PAN India.

Will my customers see the Rhinocult name?

No. Orders ship under your brand with your labels and packaging.

Can I connect my online store?

Yes. We work with Shopify stores. Book a call and we will walk you through the setup.

Do you support Cash on Delivery?

Yes. We support COD orders and settle payouts quickly.

How do I get started?

Book a call or message us on WhatsApp. We will understand your brand and send you a sample plan.
